For your school

Share from your classroom

If you teach, counsel or lead in a K-12 school, this short form asks what you see: which Computer Science courses your school offers, roughly who takes them, what gets in the way, and whether anything has changed since 2025. It takes about five minutes, one section at a time.

How your answers will be used

  • Nothing is public until it is reviewed. Every response is saved as pending, and the site's author reads it before anything appears.
  • Approved answers appear in summary form. That means counts across many responses on the From the classroom page. Any count under 10 is hidden so a small school can't be picked out.
  • A short quote may appear as a card, copied word for word from your written answers. Your name and school appear with it only if you check the box that allows it.
  • Your email is private. It is used only to follow up with you, and it is never shown on the site or returned by any public API.
  • Please do not name students or include anything that could identify a student.
  • This site is a class project for ESPHE 3206 at The Ohio State University. The answers are self-reported and are not a representative sample.

The form needs JavaScript turned on.
