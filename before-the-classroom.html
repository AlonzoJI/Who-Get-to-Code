Before the classroom

Class shapes who has seen computer science before Intro CS

Long before a student can sign up for Intro or AP Computer Science, family income shapes how much computing they have seen at home and in the programs around school. A 2015 Google and Gallup survey found lower-income students had the least access to computer science learning at school. By high school, that gap shows up in who is sitting in the class.
