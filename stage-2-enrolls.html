Who enrolls in high school computer science?

The Civil Rights Data Collection asked public schools to count students enrolled in at least one computer science course in grades 9–12 or the ungraded equivalent at the fall 2023 snapshot. These charts show reported enrollment nationwide and in Ohio.

Enrollment by race and sex

The charts have different count scales. The public file provides male and female counts for these fields and suppresses the nonbinary fields.

How the counts were calculated

Each cell sums reports from schools that recorded at least one computer science class. The national aggregation covers the 50 states and D.C. The collection includes introductory and advanced classes, including AP and IB courses. The pipeline’s enrollment rate still needs the number of high school students in each group who could take a course at their school.
