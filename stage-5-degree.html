Who earns a computer science bachelor’s degree?

IPEDS reports bachelor’s awards by field of study, race, ethnicity and sex. This chart covers computer and information sciences and support services, the federal CIP 11 category.

Who receives the awards

Sex and race or ethnicity are separate breakdowns. Each uses the same total for first-major bachelor’s awards. The pipeline’s completion rate needs a separate source that follows the same students from college entry to graduation.

IPEDS counts awards by first major. The race and ethnicity breakdown includes U.S. nonresidents and awards with race or ethnicity unknown.
