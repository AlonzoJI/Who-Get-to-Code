ColorStack and why I built this

Student-run communities give computer science students a place to get help with homework, meet peers who look like them and hear about internships from people who just went through it. ColorStack is a national nonprofit whose mission is “to increase the number of Black and Latinx Computer Science graduates that go on to launch rewarding technical careers.” The author of this site helped start its chapter at Ohio State.

Source: ColorStack, About Us, accessed Nov. 21, 2026

What changes for chapters like this one

How student-run groups can operate

Ohio State treats registered student organizations as separate from the university, with students making decisions and running activities. Its Senate Bill 1 guidance says colleges can still provide funding and administrative support on equal terms across groups. Registered organizations must keep membership open regardless of protected-class status. Faculty and staff advisors can help with operations while students decide what the group does.

Source: Ohio State’s SB 1 implementation guide, Registered Student Organizations

ColorStack at Ohio State can keep its mission of helping more Black and Latinx computer science students launch technical careers while welcoming eligible undergraduates of any race or ethnicity. Its constitution already sets out open membership on those terms. Ohio State listed the chapter in its Autumn 2026 student involvement fair materials.

Sources: ColorStack at Ohio State constitution, Articles II–III and Ohio State Student Activities, Autumn 2026 fair directory
