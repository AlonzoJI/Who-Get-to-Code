What changed after 2023

Since 2023, programs built to widen the computer science pipeline have been rolled back in four places: the courts, the federal government, Ohio law and tech companies. A data problem

The end of diversity reports is itself a loss of outcome data

Google, Meta and Microsoft's annual diversity reports were one of the few public records of who gets hired in tech. When they stopped publishing in 2025, stage 06 of the pipeline became harder to track. Without those reports, people outside the companies can't easily tell whether hiring gaps are closing or growing.
