The rollback

What changed after 2023

Since 2023, programs built to widen the computer science pipeline have been rolled back in four places: the courts, the federal government, Ohio law and tech companies. Select an event to see what happened, which stage of the pipeline it touches, what supporters of the change argue and what the outcome data shows so far.

The technical version

Each dot sits at the date of the event. Ranges, like Meta and Google's changes in January and February 2025, sit at their first date. Hollow dots are outcome data rather than a policy change. Every fact, date and quote was checked against the linked source; caveats are in each card's technical version.

"What supporters argue" quotes the people or institutions that made the change, in their own words. Where a source gives no public reason, the card says so.
