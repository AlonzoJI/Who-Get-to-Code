What happened at elite colleges after the 2023 ruling

In June 2023 the Supreme Court ended race-conscious admissions in SFFA v. Harvard. The class that started college in fall 2023 was the last one admitted under the old rules. Comparing it with fall 2025 shows what the first two classes admitted under the ruling look like at the most selective colleges.

A second count, from a larger set of colleges

The technical version

Bloomberg and Higher Ed Today count different sets of colleges (27 and 29) and don't name them all, so their numbers are shown side by side, not combined. Bloomberg measures Black students as a share of the first-year class but says only "enrollment" for Hispanic students. Higher Ed Today doesn't say which year it compares fall 2025 with. The before-and-after averages stay pending until they are computed from one dataset and checked.
