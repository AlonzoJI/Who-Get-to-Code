The technical version

Who answered. Anyone who opens the form can answer it, so the people who respond are the ones who heard about it and chose to. These counts describe them, not schools in general, and should not be read as rates for any state or for the country.

What is counted. Only responses the site's author approved, and only from people who agreed to publication in summary form. Courses and barriers allow more than one answer, so those bars do not add up to the total.

Small counts. Any count under 10 is hidden, and so is the total until there are at least 10 approved responses. On the one-answer question about changes since 2025, if only one answer would be hidden, the next smallest is hidden too, so the hidden count can't be worked out from the total.

Quotes. Each quote is copied word for word from a response the author chose to feature. A quote card lists the role and grade band. It lists the state only when at least 10 approved responses come from that state, and a name or school only when the educator allowed it. Emails are never published.
